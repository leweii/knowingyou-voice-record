**You are responsible for ensuring your use of this app complies with applicable law.** In many jurisdictions, recording a meeting or call without participants' consent carries legal risk (some places require "one-party consent," others require "all-party consent"). Before recording, please:

- Clearly inform all participants that the meeting will be recorded and obtain any consent required;
